# T045: Burger Task Backlog Sections

Status: implemented in V1 foundation.

## Goal

Use the existing burger-bar Tasks section as the wider week/backlog surface instead of adding a full calendar week view.

## Scope

- Group tasks into planned today, this week backlog, next week backlog, someday/suggestions, blocked/waiting, and background/phased work.
- Show date-intent and scheduling badges so hidden tasks explain why they are not on Today.
- Keep the day-by-day navigator as the way to inspect a specific day.

## Acceptance Criteria

- "Sometime next week" tasks are visible in the Tasks panel.
- Tasks not planned today still show their date intent/status.
- Background/phased/concurrent work is visible outside the timeline.
